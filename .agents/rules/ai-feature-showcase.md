# AI Features & Showcase Maintenance Rule

## Core Requirement
Every time a new AI function is added, updated, or enhanced in ClassCare 360:
1. You **MUST** update `src/components/dashboard/AiFeatureShowcase.tsx` (`AI_FEATURES` list) to include the new/updated AI feature.
2. Clearly describe:
   - **Feature Name (Thai & English)**
   - **Badge & Category** (`ocr` | `teaching` | `assistant`)
   - **ปัญหาเดิมที่คุณครูเจอ (Pain point)**
   - **สิ่งที่ AI ช่วยคุณครู (AI solution & selling point)**
   - **Tags & Highlights**
   - **Direct CTA button & route link** (`targetPath`)
3. Ensure this showcase section on the Dashboard always acts as the primary marketing and feature discovery showcase for users.
